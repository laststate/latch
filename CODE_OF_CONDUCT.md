# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Latch a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, and exclusion are not.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- being specific, patient, and respectful in technical discussion;
- assuming good intent while still asking for evidence;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty, hardware limits, and mistakes;
- helping new contributors find enough context to make progress;
- prioritizing the safety and privacy of people affected by captured device data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community chat, events, and public spaces where someone represents Latch.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a [private repository advisory](https://github.com/laststate/latch/security/advisories/new), with `Code of Conduct` at the start of the report. Do not open a public issue about a sensitive incident.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as possible, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban someone when necessary. Anyone with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from handling the report.
