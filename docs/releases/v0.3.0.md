# Latch v0.3.0

Latch v0.3.0 turns the public runtime from a crash-evidence baseline into a
broader embedded mission-observability and product-qualification subsystem.
It remains diagnostic infrastructure: vehicle safe-state, abort/ascent logic,
and certification stay outside Latch and must be independently implemented and
qualified on the final product.

## Major additions

- retained CRC-protected black-box recorder with quiet/normal/anomaly profiles;
- mission, dive, node, mode and cross-node incident correlation context;
- synchronized UTC evidence with RTC/GNSS/NTP/PTP/host source tracking;
- stronger build-aware crash/event fingerprinting and fleet crash clustering;
- health supervisor for watchdog, power, heap, spool, task deadlines, leak,
  environment sensor faults and vibration evidence;
- AUV environment samples for pressure, depth, humidity, temperature, leak and vibration;
- task/IRQ/mutex/DMA/link/state breadcrumbs for deterministic incident reconstruction;
- provisioning lifecycle, secure-element attestation, key rotation/revocation and
  fail-closed secure decommissioning;
- generic authenticated A/B OTA orchestration with monotonic anti-rollback state;
- named fault-injection points wired into storage, spool, transport and ACK paths;
- Critical and Emergency spool reservations plus priority-first store-and-forward;
- fleet SQLite indexing, build comparison, crash-regression detection and canary
  promotion guardrails;
- deterministic, secret-filtering remote support bundles and symbol lookup tooling;
- concurrent black-box producer tests, replay property tests and expanded power-loss tests;
- production coverage gate over `src/`, `arch/` and `ports/` at >=93% line and
  >=80% branch coverage, plus sanitizer, static-analysis and mutation gates;
- Commercial AUV safety boundary, FMEA/FTA starters and real-time/endurance
  qualification worksheets.

## Qualification boundary

The release is suitable for product integration and qualification, not a claim
that any particular AUV is certified. The exact board, MCU, flash, bootloader,
compiler, secure element, power system and environmental envelope still require
physical HIL, pressure/thermal/vibration/EMC/power-cut testing and independent
security/safety review.
